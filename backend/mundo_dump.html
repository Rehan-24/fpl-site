MINI LEAGUE NEWS ROUNDUP

The original FPL mini-league newspaper! Enter your league number in the box above for stories and stats about your mini-league. Supports classic leagues and head-to-head leagues (work-in-progress!) of 3-100 managers.

Please note the league number is different to the league invite code and will be digits/numbers only. For help finding your league number click the purple question mark icon above - if you have any trouble you can also email me at hello@fplmundo.com.

PREMATCH EDITION

Exclusively for FPL Mundo Patreon supporter leagues - Prematch Editions contain transfer news, chip usage, fixtures and more and are available around 1 hour after each FPL gameweek deadline (ie often around 12pm UK on a Saturday). It also includes the Transfer Summary chart to keep abreast of all the league's transfer business, and the Inplay Points chart - the quickest way to check which of your rivals own Lewis Dunk.

POSTMATCH EDITION

The classic Postmatch Edition has all the reaction, highlights and lowlights from the gameweek just gone. Free for mini-leagues of 3-50 managers! Normally available the morning after the final match of a gameweek (usually Mondays or Tuesdays.)
